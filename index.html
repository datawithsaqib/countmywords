Reading & speaking time

Reading time uses about 200 words per minute. Speaking time uses about 130 words per minute — a normal speaking speed. Both update live in the stats above as you type.

Useful checks: a 1,000-word article ≈ 5 minutes to read, ≈ 7–8 minutes to speak aloud.

BotKeep or free GitHub? — the verdict

Use GitHub Pages (free). BotKeep is not the better choice for this site. Here is the simple reason: this site is just HTML, CSS and JavaScript files. It needs no server at all. BotKeep rents you a small server for bots and apps — power this site never uses — and it is still in beta with no uptime promise. GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages and Netlify are built exactly for static sites like this one: free, fast, and reliable.

Why text tools won — the final conclusion

The final research compared three finalists head-to-head. Text tools ranked 1st, image tools 2nd, and a niche affiliate site 3rd. Text tools won by elimination — it is the only one that passes the traffic test with small competitors, has the simplest build, and is hardest for AI search answers to replace (an AI can state a word count for a quote, but it cannot count your pasted text).

The honest caveat — do not skip this: no finalist passed every test cleanly. The best small-site evidence implies only about $0.70 per 1,000 pageviews for a geographically mixed word-counter site. At that rate, $50–$200 a month needs roughly 70,000–290,000 pageviews a month (about 45,000–190,000 visits). Realistic time to $50–$200 a month is 9–18 months; 6–12 months is the optimistic floor. If you need $50–$200 certainly within 6–12 months, none of the three options guarantees it.

Why image tools lost (2nd)

  • Traffic exists, but the biggest small example (compressor.io, ~180,000–295,000 visits) is 82–85% direct/brand traffic — built over years, not winnable from Google by a new site.
  • Advertisers pay almost nothing: CPC of $0.01–$0.05 on core terms; implied ~$0.38 per 1,000 pageviews, so $50–$200 needs ~132,000–526,000 pageviews.
  • Hardest code: browser image codecs, EXIF rotation, AVIF/HEIC gaps, mobile memory crashes.
  • A $5 premium needs accounts and card payments — a second product, against the $0-start rule.

Why the affiliate site lost (3rd)

  • Failed the traffic test outright: no small affiliate competitor with published traffic was found at all.
  • Its sources assume 80–100 articles, not 10–20 reviews — too much work at 1 hour a day.
  • Highest wipe-out risk: in one study, 27% of 7,105 niche sites lost 91%+ of traffic in a Google update period; the named case, HouseFresh, fell 91% and took ~2 years to recover.
  • Amazon closes a new affiliate account that makes no 3 qualifying sales in 180 days.

Week-1 go / no-go test — run this before buying a domain

Seven days, 1 hour a day, free tools only. Days 1–2: search these 12 long-tail phrases in a private Google window and note who ranks in the top 5: character counter without spaces · sentence counter online · case converter snake case · reading time calculator · word counter for essays · how many pages is 1000 words · character counter for twitter 280 · uppercase to lowercase converter · paragraph counter · word counter no signup · speaking time calculator · title case converter online. Days 3–4: test this prototype against Microsoft Word / Google Docs on 5 samples, test it on a phone, and ask 5 real students or writers if they would return. Days 5–7: decide using the rules below.

GO  Build it, if ALL are true

  • At least 6 of 12 phrases have a non-giant site in the top 5
  • At most 6 of 12 are fully answered by an AI Overview with no need to paste text
  • At least 2 small competitors in those results have 20%+ US/UK/CA/AU traffic
  • The prototype's counts match Word/Docs on at least 4 of 5 samples, and it is fast on a phone
  • At least 3 of 5 test users say they would come back
  • You can name 6 pages, each with its own specific long-tail angle

NO-GO  Stop, if ANY is true

  • Giants fill the top 3 on 9+ of 12 phrases, with no small site in the top 5
  • Small competitors' traffic is 60%+ from low-paying countries (ad rate will be under $1)
  • The prototype counts wrong, or test users would not return
  • You cannot find 6 different long-tail angles — the space is already taken

If it is NO-GO: stop and pick a different niche with the same test. Do not fall back to image tools or affiliate without re-testing them — they scored worse.

Your 30-day plan

One hour a day. One tool at a time. Nothing is bought and nothing extra is built in the first 30 days — no premium, no accounts, no image tools, no affiliate pages, no second site.

Days 1–7 · Test first

Run the week-1 test above

Do the 12-phrase Google check, the accuracy test against Word/Docs, the phone test, and the 5-user test. Buy the domain only if the result is GO — one .com, about $10–15/year.

Days 8–14 · Build the core — 3 pages

Word Counter + Character Counter + Case Converter

Put this hub live on GitHub Pages (free). Each tool gets its own page, its own title, and 600–900 words of genuinely useful text — how the counting works, character limits, worked examples. Add About, Contact and Privacy pages (needed for AdSense later), a sitemap, and Google Search Console. This page you are reading is the working prototype for all three tools.

Days 15–21 · Add 2 more pages

Sentence Counter + Reading-Time / Speaking-Time Calculator

Link all tools together from one hub page and from each other. Fix any counting mistakes that show up. Do not add image tools. Do not add affiliate content.

Days 22–30 · Submit and measure

Apply for AdSense only now — then follow the data

Apply for AdSense only once all 5 pages are live, indexed by Google, and carry real text (thin pages get rejected). Check Search Console impressions and clicks daily, inside your 1 hour. Add 1–2 more long-tail pages only if a page is already getting impressions. Day-30 decision: if impressions are still ~0 after indexing, re-check the week-1 search results before building anything more.

After day 30 — the honest timeline

  • First $1 earned: month 2–4 if a page ranks and AdSense is approved; month 4–6 is more honest for a brand-new domain.
  • $50–$200/month: 9–18 months realistic. 6–12 months only if traffic is mostly US/UK/CA/AU and Journey accepts the site.
  • Growth rule: one new tool page at a time, only where Google already shows demand. Accuracy is the product — a counter that counts wrong loses everyone.
