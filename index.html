What is CountMyWords?

CountMyWords is a free set of small text tools for students, writers, teachers and anyone who works with words. The word counter checks the length of essays, articles, assignments and posts. The character counter checks limits for social media and search descriptions. The case converter fixes text typed in the wrong case, and the reading time calculator gives a quick estimate of how long a text takes to read.

Every tool runs in your browser. There is no account to create and nothing to install. Paste your text, read the numbers, and copy the result when you are done.

How words are counted

Words are groups of letters and numbers. A word with an apostrophe, such as don’t, counts as one word, and a hyphenated word, such as well-known, also counts as one word. Numbers count as words, and extra spaces or blank lines do not create extra words.

Frequently asked questions

Is CountMyWords free?

Yes. All the tools on this site are free to use, with no account and no sign-up.

Does my text leave my device?

No. The counting happens in your browser, on your own device. Your text is not uploaded to a server, saved, or shared with anyone.

How are words counted?

Words are groups of letters and numbers. Contractions such as don’t and hyphenated words such as well-known count as one word each. Numbers count as words. Multiple spaces and blank lines do not add words.

How are characters counted?

Characters are counted as visible characters. An emoji such as 😀 counts as one character, not two, so “Hello 😀” is 7 characters including the space and 6 without it. You can see both counts — with spaces and without spaces — on the Character Counter page.

What is the reading time based on?

Reading time is an estimate based on about 200 words per minute, and speaking time on about 130 words per minute. Real times vary with the text and the reader, so treat the numbers as a guide, not an exact prediction.
