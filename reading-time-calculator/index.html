How the estimates work

These are estimates, not exact predictions. Reading time is based on about 200 words per minute, and speaking time on about 130 words per minute, which is a normal speaking speed. Real times vary with the text, the topic and the reader — for example, a 1,000-word article takes about 5 minutes to read and about 7–8 minutes to speak aloud.
