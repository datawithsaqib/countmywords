How characters are counted

Characters are counted as visible characters. An emoji such as 😀 counts as one character, not two, and combined characters and flags are counted the same way. “Characters” includes spaces, line breaks and punctuation. “Chars, no spaces” counts the same text with all spaces removed.

For example, “Hello 😀” is 7 characters including the space and 6 characters without it.
