How words are counted

Words are groups of letters and numbers. A word with an apostrophe, such as don’t, counts as one word, and a hyphenated word, such as well-known, also counts as one word. Numbers count as words, and text in other languages counts too. Extra spaces, line breaks and blank lines do not create extra words.

For example, “Hello world!” is 2 words, 12 characters including the space, and 1 sentence.

How to use this word counter for essays

A word counter for essays is a simple check you can use before you hand in your work. Paste your essay into the box above, or type it there directly. The word count updates as you type, so you can see at once if you are under or over the limit. When you are done, use the Copy button to copy the text, or Clear to start again. There is no sign-up, and your text stays on your device.

Start by pasting a full draft, not just one paragraph. That way the count includes your introduction, your main points and your conclusion. If your teacher gave you a word limit, compare the number at the top with that limit before you edit. It is easier to cut a long draft down than to add words to a short one at the end.

Check the other numbers too. The character count helps if your essay is pasted into an online form with a limit. The sentence and paragraph counts help you see the shape of your work. If you have only two very long paragraphs, your ideas may be packed too tightly. If you have many very short paragraphs, your argument may be broken up too much.

Word limits for essays and assignments

Word limits are set by your teacher or your school, so always follow the limit you were given. The numbers below are common examples, not rules for every class. A short school essay is often about 500 words. A longer school essay is often 800 to 1,000 words. A college essay or a short assignment is often 1,000 to 1,500 words. A longer assignment or a report may be 2,000 to 3,000 words. If your limit is different, use the number you were given.

Most limits allow a small amount of give and take, but do not rely on that. Some teachers count the words in the main text only, and do not count the title, the list of sources, or a table. Others count everything. If you are not sure, ask your teacher which parts count. Then check your draft with this tool and keep the main text inside the limit.

How many pages is 1000 words?

This is one of the most common questions about essay length, and the answer depends on spacing. As a rough guide, 1,000 words is about 2 pages if the text is single-spaced, and about 4 pages if it is double-spaced. The same guide works for other lengths: 500 words is about 1 page single-spaced or 2 pages double-spaced, and 2,000 words is about 4 pages single-spaced or 8 pages double-spaced.

Font size, margins and line spacing change the result, so these are estimates only. A larger font or wider margins will make the same 1,000 words fill more pages. A smaller font will make it fill fewer pages. When a teacher asks for a word count, use the word count, not the page count, because the word count does not change when you change the font.

Why count words, and how to stay within the limit

Counting words helps you plan. If your limit is 1,000 words and you have three main points, you can plan about 300 words for each point and about 100 words for the introduction and conclusion together. That plan is easier to follow than writing without a target and then trying to cut the text down.

If you are over the limit, look for repeated ideas, long examples that say the same thing twice, and sentences that could be shorter. If you are under the limit, look for a point that needs more evidence or a clearer example. Do not add filler sentences just to reach the number. A clear essay that is a little short is better than a long essay that repeats itself.

Paste the text, check the number, edit, and check again. That is the whole process, and it takes less than a minute.
