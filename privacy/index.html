Privacy Policy

Short version: your text stays on your device. We do not collect it.

Text you type or paste

All counting and conversion on CountMyWords happens in your browser, on your own device. The text you type or paste is not uploaded to a server, saved, or shared with anyone. Closing the page removes it from view; the site does not store it.

Accounts

There are no accounts, sign-ups or logins on this site, so we do not hold account details for visitors.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use its own cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. It loads no external fonts or scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may collect standard server logs, such as the pages requested, as part of hosting the site. We do not control those logs.

Links to other sites

This site links to its public GitHub repository. If you follow that link, GitHub’s own privacy policy applies there.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. If you have questions, please use the contact page.
