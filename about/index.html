About CountMyWords

A small, free set of text tools for students, writers, teachers and anyone who works with words.

CountMyWords started as a simple idea: counting words should be quick, free and private. The site offers a word counter, a character counter, a case converter, a sentence counter and a reading time calculator. Each tool does one job, and does it in your browser.

There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no subscriptions. You paste your text, read the numbers, and copy the result if you need it. Your text is never uploaded to a server or stored by the site.

The site is a work in progress. If a count looks wrong, or a tool does not behave as you expect, please let us know through the contact page.
